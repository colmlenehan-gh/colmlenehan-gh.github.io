05 / The principles

A way of working.

Make it
matter.

A few things I try to keep in mind, whatever I am building.

  1. 01

    Keep it simple.

    Clear ideas. Less noise. Nothing without a reason.

  2. 02

    Make it useful.

    Start with what something should do for someone.

  3. 03

    Respect privacy.

    Consider the person on the other side of the screen.

  4. 04

    Care about the details.

    Small decisions add up to how something feels.

  5. 05

    Build, learn, improve.

    Make a start. Find the weaknesses. Keep working.
